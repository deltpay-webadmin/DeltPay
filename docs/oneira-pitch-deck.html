The science

Two replicated findings.
One engineering wrapper.

Scent reaches the dreaming brain

Odors presented during sleep influence memory consolidation and dream emotion — smell is the only sense with a direct shortcut to the brain's emotion and memory centers, and it doesn't wake the sleeper. (Rasch et al., Science, 2007; Schredl et al., 2009)

Dream content can be steered

MIT Media Lab's Dormio demonstrated targeted dream incubation: audio prompts at sleep onset reliably inserted chosen themes into reported dreams. (Horowitz et al., 2020; Carr et al., Consciousness & Cognition, 2020)

Positioning discipline: Oneira is wellness, not medicine — "designed to inspire the dreams you want," never "treats" anything. IFRA-compliant fragrance, cool-air atomization, zero medical claims. The promise is calibrated to the evidence: thematic influence and better recall, not scripted dreams — and the journal lets every user see their own hit rate.
