## Posting body

### Winners don't wait to get paid. Neither should you.

Delt is the all-in-one platform Main Street businesses use to run, grow, and fund their operations — 0% card processing, point-of-sale, same-day funding, working capital, websites, and AI analytics. We win deals against Stripe, Square, and legacy processors every week, and we're building a national team of independent sales agents to sign the next thousand merchants.

**We'll be straight with you, because that's how we do business.** Our company motto with merchants is that we tell the truth about fees, terms, and eligibility — even when it costs us the deal. Same rule applies to this posting: this is a **1099, commission-only role with no base salary and no benefits.** The first 90 days are hard. If you need a paycheck next Friday, this is not your job. If you want to build a residual book you own, keep reading — the math below is real.

### What you earn

- **$150 activation bonus** for every merchant account you sign that activates and processes its first batch — **$250** when the account also takes our KORONA POS or Delt Capital funding.
- **Lifetime residuals: start at a 50% split and earn your way to 70%.** You keep a share of the monthly revenue on every account you sign, for as long as that merchant processes with Delt. Tier 1 starts at 50% — most ISOs start agents at 30–40%. Produce, and you move up the ladder to 60% and 70%.
- **Residuals are never clawed back.** The only clawback in the entire program: the activation bonus, and only if the merchant cancels within 90 days.
- **Multiple commissions per relationship.** Processing residuals, POS placements, Delt Capital fundings, and website/software subscriptions each pay — one merchant can generate several income streams.
- **Paid on the 15th, every month**, with a per-merchant statement in your agent portal. You see every account, every month, down to the penny.

Realistic numbers, not hype: an agent signing ~6 accounts a month is around **$1,700/month by month 3** and **$3,900–$4,500/month by month 12** (roughly $31,000–$40,000 in year one), then starts year two with a residual base already paying before they sell anything. Slower pace earns less; our top producers earn more. Full assumptions and scenario math are in the comp plan we send every serious applicant.

### What we give you (that most ISOs don't)

- **A live residual dashboard.** Your own portal showing every merchant, every month's residuals, and every commission — no "trust me" statements, no mystery math.
- **An AI closing tool.** Upload a prospect's Stripe or Square statement and our analyzer builds the savings comparison and quote in minutes, not days.
- **Real sales enablement.** A library of tested talk tracks and objection responses for cash-discount selling, plus a cost calculator you can run live in front of a merchant.
- **Something to sell in almost any door.** 0% processing, free hardware on our entry plan, high-risk approvals (CBD, vape, liquor, nutraceuticals — merchants the aggregators shut down), POS, capital, and websites.
- **A real title and identity.** Account Manager title, @delt email, and business cards — you represent a platform, not a rate sheet.
- **A path up.** Tier promotions to 60% and 70%, and a Sub-ISO track for agents who want to recruit and build their own team on our infrastructure.
- **Founders' Circle.** Top producers get portal leaderboard recognition and a quarterly dinner with the founding team in Miami.
- **Your business, your rules.** Set your own schedule, work your own market anywhere in the US. No quotas to keep the book you've already built.

### Who thrives here

- Reps with commission-only or merchant-services/ISO experience who are tired of 35% splits and opaque residual statements
- Closers with a network — restaurant, retail, salon, auto, or high-risk operators — and a concrete plan for their first ten signings
- Self-managers who treat a 1099 role like the business it is

### Who shouldn't apply

- Anyone who needs immediate, guaranteed income — there is no base salary and no draw
- Anyone expecting leads to be handed to them — producing agents get inbound referrals shared with them, but prospecting is the job

### Requirements

- US-based and authorized to work in the United States
- Sales experience preferred; merchant services / payments / ISO experience a strong plus
- Phone, computer, and the discipline to run your own pipeline

### The legal fine print

This is an independent contractor (1099) position. It is 100% commission — no base salary, no hourly pay, and no employee benefits (no health insurance, retirement plan, or paid time off). Contractors are responsible for their own taxes. Residual and bonus terms are defined in the Delt agent agreement; "lifetime residuals" means for as long as the merchant continues processing with Delt under that agreement. Earnings depend on individual production; the figures above are modeled scenarios, not guarantees. Delt is an equal opportunity company.
