## Posting body

### Compounding residual income

Delt is a merchant services platform for small and medium businesses — payment processing, same-day funding, working capital, websites, and AI analytics. We win deals against Stripe, Square, and legacy processors every week, and we're building a national team of independent agents to sign the next thousand merchants.

We'll be straight with you, because that's how we do business with merchants too: this is a **1099, commission-only role with no base salary.** The first 90 days are hard. If you need a paycheck next Friday, this is not your job. If you want to build a residual book you own, the math below is real.

### What you earn

- **$150–$1,000 activation bonus per account**, based on merchant size. Every account that activates and processes its first batch pays. Big deals pay like big deals.
- **Lifetime residuals: 50% split on day one, up to 70% as you produce.** You keep a share of the monthly revenue on every account you sign, for as long as that merchant processes with Delt. Most ISOs start agents at 30–40%. We start you at 50.
- **3% of every Delt Capital funding.** Get a merchant a $50,000 working-capital deal and that's $1,500 — renewals pay too. Your book funds itself twice.
- **Fast Start: up to $1,500 in your first 90 days.** 3 activations in 30 days → +$500. 10 in 90 → +$1,000.
- **Momentum kicker:** 8+ activations in a calendar month and every one retroactively pays +$50.
- **Residuals are never clawed back.** The only clawback in the program is the activation bonus, and only if the merchant cancels within 90 days.
- **Paid on the 15th, every month**, with a per-merchant statement in your agent portal — every account, every month, down to the penny.

Realistic numbers, not hype: an agent signing ~6 accounts a month earns around **$2,000 in month one**, about **$2,100/month by month 3**, and **$4,300–$4,800/month by month 12** — roughly **$38,000–$45,000 in year one** — then starts year two with a residual base paying before they sell anything. Capital deals stack on top. Slower pace earns less; top producers earn more. Full assumptions are in the comp plan we send every serious applicant.

### Bring your people

- **Recruit an agent, get paid on their book.** Earn a **10% override on the residual production of every agent you personally recruit** — paid from Delt's share, never from theirs — for as long as you're both active. Plus **$250 cash** when your recruit hits their 5th activation.
- One level, no fees, ever. Nobody pays to join and nobody earns off strangers — you get paid for bringing in producers you know and helping them win.

### What you get

- **A live residual dashboard.** Every merchant, every month, every commission — no "trust me" statements.
- **An AI closing tool.** Upload a prospect's Stripe or Square statement; our analyzer builds the savings comparison and quote in minutes.
- **A closer in your corner.** For your first 90 days, bring a live merchant to the table and a senior closer joins the call with you. You keep the full commission.
- **Weekly live deal lab.** Real deals on the table with the founding team — pricing, objections, and what's closing right now.
- **Account Manager title, @delt email, business cards, and a Delt gear kit.** You represent a platform, not a rate sheet.
- **Delt for your own business, free.** Run your own operation on our platform — website included — at no cost while you're active.
- **A wellness package for active producers:** gym membership reimbursed, telehealth access, and healthcare enrollment support — earned by staying in production (3+ activations in the trailing 90 days).
- **President's Club.** Top producers join the founding team in Miami every quarter.
- **Your business, your rules.** Your hours, your market, anywhere in the US. No quotas to keep the book you've built.

### Don't want to sell? Become a Referral Partner

Know business owners but don't want to run a pipeline? Send us the introduction and we do everything else — the meetings, the quote, the paperwork, the follow-up. You earn **15% of the monthly revenue on that account for the life of the account**, paid on the 15th with the same per-merchant statement our agents get. No quota, no minimum, no cap on how many you refer. Mention "referral partner" when you apply.

### Who thrives here

- Reps with commission-only or merchant-services/ISO experience who are tired of 35% splits and opaque residual statements
- Closers with a network — restaurant, retail, salon, auto, or high-risk operators — and a concrete plan for their first ten signings

### Who shouldn't apply

- Anyone who needs immediate, guaranteed income — there is no base salary and no draw
- Anyone expecting leads to be handed to them — producing agents get inbound referrals shared with them, but prospecting is the job

### Requirements

- US-based and authorized to work in the United States
- Sales experience preferred; merchant services / payments / ISO experience a strong plus
- Phone, computer, and the discipline to run your own pipeline

### The legal fine print

This is an independent contractor (1099) position. It is 100% commission — no base salary, no hourly pay, and no employee benefits (no health insurance, retirement plan, or paid time off). The wellness package and other programs described above are production-based contractor rewards under the Delt agent agreement, not employment benefits. Contractors are responsible for their own taxes. Residual, bonus, override, and referral terms are defined in the Delt agent and referral partner agreements; "lifetime residuals" and "life of the account" mean for as long as the merchant continues processing with Delt under those agreements. The recruiting override is single-level and production-based: it applies only to agents you personally recruit, and no fee is ever charged to join. Earnings depend on individual production; the figures above are modeled scenarios, not guarantees. Delt is an equal opportunity company.
